Limitations

  • About six weeks of data, so attendance history is short. Years of NHS records would likely make history features much stronger.
  • SMS reminders were mostly sent for appointments booked further ahead, so their effect is mixed up with lead time. Measuring reminder impact needs a controlled trial.
  • Neighbourhood likely reflects deprivation or travel distance. Use scores to offer support such as reminders or transport help, never to overbook or penalise.
  • Calibration brings the average in line, but the riskiest tenth still runs a few points above what happened. Treat the very top scores as a ranking more than an exact chance.
  • Some patients have several bookings on one day, and each earlier day counts toward their history. This inflates "previous no-shows" for a few patients and pushes them to the top of the list.
